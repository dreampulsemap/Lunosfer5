Write down your dream and discover what it may be telling you, in seconds. Lunosfer brings AI dream interpretation, a dream journal and vision boards together in one place.

🌙 AI DREAM INTERPRETATION
Describe your dream in your own words. Drawing on the approaches of Freud and Jung, Lunosfer finds the symbols, the dominant archetype and the emotional tone of your dream. The "In Plain Words" section sums up what it may mean for you. If your dream is very short, it asks you questions; tap "Add to your dream" to add details and get a fresh reading.

🎨 AN IMAGE FOR EVERY DREAM
Every dream gets its own image. Over time your dream archive becomes your personal night gallery.

🔮 DEEP ANALYSIS
An in-depth report across your dreams: recurring themes, your fear map, a personality reading and an exercise made for you.

🧭 DAILY COMPASS & DIARY
Start each morning with an intention from the Daily Compass. Capture moments in your diary and share them as stories with friends if you like.

✨ VISION BOARDS
Turn your goals into visual vision boards, build a step-by-step roadmap and track your progress.

🏆 JOURNEY
Earn XP as you log dreams and write in your diary; rank up, complete daily quests and collect badges.

👥 COMMUNITY
Explore dreams from around the world, like and comment. Message friends and share your dreams on WhatsApp, Instagram or X.

🔒 YOUR PRIVACY, YOUR CHOICE
Your dreams are private by default. You decide what to share.

🌍 14 LANGUAGES
English, Turkish, German, Spanish, French, Russian, Arabic, Japanese and more.

Lunosfer is not fortune-telling: it helps you understand your dreams through a psychological lens and get to know yourself better. It is not a substitute for medical or psychological diagnosis or treatment.
